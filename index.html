Abstract

I am a graduate student in the Department of Physics at POSTECH, working on machine learning for physics.

I care about both halves of that. One is applying learning methods to concrete physical problems. The other is understanding why they work at all — what a trained model has actually represented about a system, and how far its answers can be trusted.
