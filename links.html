Rule and mark: a matrix product state. Each circle is a tensor; a line between two circles is a bond index contracted between them, and a line with a free end is a physical index. The rule is an infinite, translation-invariant chain; the mark is a five-site chain in mixed-canonical form, its orthogonality centre filled. The photo plate, when empty, shows a PEPS, the same network in two dimensions. Set in Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Mono.
